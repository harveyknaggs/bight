The story

Rolleston is my home.

I've been passionate about the art of barbering since the very first time I stepped into a barbershop. I can still remember the smell, the decor, and the vibe of that place. It left a mark on me.

I noticed most men's haircuts were being done too quickly, without care or real craftsmanship. I wanted to change that. Blackbeard Lounge is here to bring a premium vibe, a place of value where every man feels at home.

This is more than a barbershop. It's a refuge, a club where men can relax, enjoy a coffee, shoot some pool, and leave looking sharper than when they walked in.
